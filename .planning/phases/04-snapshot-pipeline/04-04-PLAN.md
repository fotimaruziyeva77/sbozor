1. `len(data)` chegaralari → `corrupt` (**dekodsiz**).
2. `data[:3] != b"\xff\xd8\xff"` yoki `data[-2:] != b"\xff\xd9"` → `corrupt`. ⚠ Izohda: bu qadam `Content-Type` **sarlavhasiga ishonmaydi** va u NVR yoki go2rtc qaytargan **HTML xato sahifasini** ham tutadi.
3. `Image.open(BytesIO(data))` → `im.draft("L", (320, 180))` → `im.load()`. `OSError`/`UnidentifiedImageError`/`DecompressionBombError` → `corrupt`. Izohda `draft` ning sababi: DCT darajasida kichraytirilgan dekod, to'liq dekoddan bir necha barobar tez va sifat metrikalari uchun 320×180 yetarli.
4. `ImageStat.Stat(im.convert("L"))` → `mean`, `stddev`.
5. To'yinganlik: kichraytirilgan RGB (`64×64` gacha) → HSV → o'rtacha `S`. ⚠ Manba **kul rang** bo'lsa (`im.mode == "L"`) to'yinganlik `0.0` — sabab izohda.
6. Qaror **aniq shu tartibda**: `blank` (faqat `stddev`) → `dark` (**ikki shart**) → `ok`. `light_mode`: `unknown` agar `corrupt`; aks holda `day`/`ir_night`/`low_light` §C.8 formulasi bilan.
