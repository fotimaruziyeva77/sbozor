# Sketch Manifest

## Design Direction

Apple-minimal jilo qatlami MAVJUD dizayn-tizim ustiga (redesign emas): iliq
"qog'oz" fon nomzodi, bitta ko'k aksent, 4 rol tipografiya + Display-XL (faqat
pul), motion tokenlari 150/250/400ms `ease-out`, vau-lahzalar Apple Pay
darajasida — sirk emas. Kassir oqimida interaktiv javob ≤150ms; skeleton
(spinner yo'q); `prefers-reduced-motion` majburiy hurmat. Manba:
`UI-UX-MASTERPLAN.md` (rang 2.0, §3 motion, §4 vau-lahzalar) va
`LANDING-BRIEF.md` (§7 "sehr" kontsepti, Realsoft'dan farqlash).

## Reference Points

Apple HIG (Clarity/Deference/Depth), Apple Pay to'lov tasdig'i, Apple mahsulot
sahifalari (scroll-hikoya). Anti-namuna: raqamli-bozor.uz (statik, byurokratik).
