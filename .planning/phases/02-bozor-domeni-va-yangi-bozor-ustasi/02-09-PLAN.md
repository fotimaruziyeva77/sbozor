* **Yagona istisno (boshlang'ich narx):** `valid_from == market_profile.operating_since` **VA** `markets.is_active = false` → 201. Sabab: Karmana bozori yillar davomida ishlab kelgan, ya'ni `operating_since` **o'tgan sana**; 02-11 dagi faollashtirish darvozasi esa har toifada `valid_from <= operating_since` bo'lgan tarifni **talab qiladi**. Istisnosiz usta 4-qadamda boshi berk ko'chaga tushardi (D-13) va MARKET-01/SC#1 API orqali bajarilmas edi.
* Istisno **ataylab tor**: sana `operating_since` ga **aynan teng** bo'lishi shart (`<=` emas) va bozor birorta marta faollashtirilmagan bo'lishi kerak. `activate` dan keyin bu tarmoq **butunlay yopiladi**; o'sha qator endi "o'tmish" bo'ladi va uni `trg_tariff_past_immutable` qulflaydi (D-07).
